Kingdom Hearts Chain of Memories — a statically recompiled game.

This package contains NO game or BIOS data. To play, place your own dumps
next to the executable:

  1. your cartridge image (.gba), sha256 6f6e7b61bb7b6ef7e2bea6e9dd01e70570a715835cc3713abe86186fd2c4e28d
2. your BIOS image as gba_bios.bin, sha256 fd2547724b505f487e6dcb29ec2ecff3af35a841a77ab2e85fd87350abd36570

Then run ./Kingdom Hearts Chain of Memories
